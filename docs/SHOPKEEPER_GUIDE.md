# Fiado: how to keep your credit book (one page)

Fiado keeps your customers' credit in a book that no one can quietly change. It runs on a **test network, so no real money moves**. You record your real credit and cash repayments as you always do; Fiado keeps the record.

**You need:** your phone (Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone) with a fingerprint, face or PIN lock, and an internet connection. Each customer needs their own phone.

Link: **https://fiado-delta.vercel.app/shop**

## 1. Open your shop (once, about 1 minute)
1. Open the link above on your phone.
2. Type your **shop name**. Keep **₦** as the currency.
3. Leave **Passkey (recommended)** selected. Tap **Create my shop**.
4. Use your fingerprint, face or PIN when the phone asks. Wait until your shop name appears. Do not close the page.
5. Tap **Copy shop key** and send it to Mustapha on WhatsApp. This is how your shop is listed. It is not a password; it is safe to share.

Use the same phone and the same browser every time. Do not clear your browser data.

## 2. Add a customer
1. Tap **Add a customer**.
2. Type the **credit limit**: the most this person can owe you at one time. The network enforces it.
3. Tap **Show join code**. A QR code appears.
4. The customer scans it with their phone camera, types **their first name or a nickname only**, and taps **Join**. They must not type a full name or a phone number. That name is visible on the public test ledger.
5. You see them on your screen. Tap **Open their line**.
6. The customer taps **Sign and join**. They now show in your book with ₦0 owed.

## 3. Record a purchase on credit
1. On the customer's row, tap **New purchase**.
2. Type what is being bought and the amount. Add a due date if you agreed one.
3. Tap **Show purchase code**. The customer scans it, unlocks with their fingerprint or PIN, reads the item and amount, and taps **Sign**.
4. Their row now shows the new amount owed. The customer's signature is the proof; you cannot make a debt for them.

If a purchase would go **over their limit**, the network refuses it and nothing is added to their tab. That refusal is counted. If you turn a customer away yourself, Fiado does not record it; write it in your notebook as before.

## 4. Record a cash repayment
1. On the customer's row, tap **Cash repayment**.
2. Type the cash you received. Tap **Record repayment**.
3. Their amount owed goes down. **Owed to you** at the top shows your total.

## What to remember
- **Use real credit and real repayments, in the order they happen.** Do not enter made-up purchases.
- **Customer privacy:** first names or nicknames only. Never phone numbers. Your own shop name and first name are shown on the Fiado evidence page only because you agreed.
- If something fails or looks wrong, take a screenshot and send it to Mustapha. Do not retry the same purchase twice.
